# Skin (Dermatological) Systems Review

## The scaffold (how to reconstruct this list under pressure)

For any skin complaint, characterise the lesion, then ask what it's doing and what it signals:

> **LESION → SYMPTOMS → CHANGE → SYSTEMIC → EXPOSURE**

| Chunk | What it probes | Questions it generates |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesion** | The morphology | New rash/lesion — where, what does it look like, distribution |
| **Symptoms** | What it does | Itch, pain, weeping, bleeding |
| **Change** | Evolution | Changing size/shape/colour of a mole; non-healing |
| **Systemic** | Internal disease | Rash + fever/joint/mucosal involvement |
| **Exposure** | Triggers | New drugs, contacts, sun, occupation, travel |

Two red-flag threads: the **changing/non-healing lesion** (skin cancer / melanoma) and the **rash-plus-systemic-upset** (drug reaction, vasculitis, meningococcaemia, connective-tissue disease).

---

## The questions

### 1. The lesion / rash — *"Tell me about the rash — where did it start, where is it now, what does it look like?"*

**Why we ask:** Distribution and morphology are the whole diagnosis in dermatology. Extensor vs flexor, symmetrical vs localised, and dermatomal patterns each point somewhere specific.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Symmetrical, extensor surfaces, well-demarcated scaly plaques** → psoriasis (ask about nails, joints, scalp)
- **Flexural, itchy, in an atopic person** → eczema/atopic dermatitis
- **Dermatomal, unilateral, painful vesicles not crossing the midline** → shingles (herpes zoster)
- **Target lesions** → erythema multiforme
- **A single well-defined patch matching contact with something** → contact dermatitis

---

### 2. Symptoms of the lesion — *"Is it itchy, painful, weeping or bleeding?"*

**Why we ask:** These qualities narrow the differential and gauge severity.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Intensely itchy** → eczema, urticaria, scabies (worse at night, burrows, contacts affected), dermatitis herpetiformis
- **Painful** → infective (cellulitis, zoster) or ischaemic
- **Bleeding/crusting/non-healing** → suspicious for skin malignancy (see below)

---

### 3. Changing / non-healing lesion — *"Have any moles or spots changed in size, shape or colour? Anything that won’t heal?"*

**Why we ask:** The skin-cancer screen. Melanoma is caught by change; keratinocyte cancers by non-healing.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Changing mole — use ABCDE (Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Colour variation, Diameter >6 mm, Evolving)** → melanoma until excluded
- **Pearly nodule with telangiectasia / rolled edge, slowly growing, sun-exposed site** → basal cell carcinoma
- **Scaly/crusted or ulcerated non-healing lesion on sun-exposed skin** → squamous cell carcinoma
- **Any non-healing lesion >4–6 weeks** → needs examination

**Red flag:** A new, changing, or non-healing pigmented lesion — never file under reassurance without looking.

---

### 4. Systemic associations — *"Any fever, joint pains, mouth or genital ulcers, or feeling generally unwell with the rash?"*

**Why we ask:** Skin is a window on internal disease; a rash with systemic upset changes the whole differential toward the dangerous.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Non-blanching purpuric rash + fever + unwell** → meningococcaemia / vasculitis — emergency red flag (do the glass/tumbler test)
- **Rash + mucosal involvement + skin peeling + new drug** → Stevens–Johnson / TEN — dermatological emergency
- **Photosensitive facial rash + joint pain + mouth ulcers + hair loss** → SLE
- **Rash + joint pain + gut symptoms** → the connective-tissue / reactive spectrum (cross-reference MSK)

**Red flag:** Non-blanching purpuric rash + fever is an emergency — do the glass test.

---

### 5. Exposure / triggers — *"Started any new medications, creams or products? Any new contacts, sun, occupation or travel?"*

**Why we ask:** So much of dermatology is external cause — drugs and contacts especially. A new drug within days–weeks of a rash is a drug eruption until proven otherwise.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **New drug + widespread symmetrical rash** → drug eruption (ask specifically — patients forget antibiotics)
- **New topical/occupational contactant + localised rash** → contact dermatitis
- **Sun exposure + rash on exposed sites** → photosensitivity (idiopathic or drug-induced)

---

## Quick-recall list (drill version)

LESION: site of onset & spread, morphology, distribution (extensor/flexor/dermatomal) · SYMPTOMS: itch, pain, weeping, bleeding · CHANGE: changing mole (ABCDE), non-healing lesion · SYSTEMIC: fever, joints, mucosal ulcers, unwell (non-blanching rash = emergency) · EXPOSURE: new drugs, contacts, sun, occupation, travel
