## Endocrine — Diabetes
- Have you ever been diagnosed with diabetes
- **Hyperglycaemia symptoms:** polyuria, polydipsia (thirst), nocturia, lethargy, blurred vision, conscious state disturbance, abdominal pain, vomiting/nausea, infections, weight change
- **Hypoglycaemia symptoms** (only relevant if on insulin, most likely skip): tremor, sweating, morning headaches, seizures, disturbance of conscious state, weight change
- **Complications:** erectile dysfunction, heart disease, peripheral vascular disease, peripheral neuropathy, renal dysfunction, visual disturbance, ulcers
- **Known diabetes:** timeframe, management, current control, complications
