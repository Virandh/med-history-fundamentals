# General / Constitutional Systems Review

## The scaffold (how to reconstruct this list under pressure)

The constitutional screen is the body's "check engine light" — non-specific signals that something systemic (malignancy, infection, inflammation, endocrine) is going on. Group them by what they signal:

> **WASTING → THERMOSTAT → ENERGY → APPETITE → LUMPS**

| Chunk | What it probes | Questions it generates |
|---|---|---|
| **Wasting** | Catabolic/malignant/chronic disease | Weight change, appetite |
| **Thermostat** | Infection, inflammation, malignancy | Fever, night sweats, rigors |
| **Energy** | Almost everything | Fatigue, malaise, sleep |
| **Appetite** | GI, malignancy, depression | Appetite, intake |
| **Lumps** | Lymphoreticular, malignancy | Lumps/bumps, itch |

This is the screen that catches the "must-not-miss" systemic diseases the organ-specific screens can miss. The **B-symptom triad** (weight loss + fever + drenching night sweats) lives here and is one of the highest-yield clusters in the systems review — it flags lymphoma and TB.

## The questions

### 1. Weight change — *"Has your weight changed recently without trying? Are your clothes looser?"*

**Why we ask:** Unintentional weight loss is one of the most important symptoms in medicine. Quantify it (kilograms, timeframe) and confirm it's unintentional. >5% body weight over 6–12 months is significant.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Unintentional weight loss + good/increased appetite** → hypermetabolic: hyperthyroidism, or uncontrolled diabetes (osmotic loss)
- **Unintentional weight loss + poor appetite** → malignancy, chronic infection (TB, HIV), depression, chronic organ failure
- **Weight gain** → fluid (cardiac/renal/liver failure), hypothyroidism, Cushing's, or simple positive energy balance
- **Rapid weight gain over days** → fluid retention, not fat

**Red flag:** unexplained weight loss is a cancer red flag at any age — pair it with the sinister screens of every organ system.

---

### 2. Fever, rigors, night sweats — *"Any fevers or shivering attacks? Drenching night sweats where you change the sheets?"*

**Why we ask:** Fever signals infection/inflammation/malignancy. **Rigors** (uncontrollable shaking) suggest bacteraemia. **Drenching** night sweats flag TB, lymphoma, and endocarditis.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Rigors + localising symptoms** → bacterial infection (pyelonephritis, cholangitis, pneumonia, abscess)
- **Drenching night sweats + weight loss + lumps** → lymphoma; + cough/travel → TB
- **Recurrent fevers with a heart murmur / IVDU / dental work** → endocarditis
- **Cyclical fevers with travel to endemic area** → malaria

---

### 3. Fatigue / malaise — *"How is your energy overall? Do you feel generally run down?"*

**Why we ask:** The most common and least specific symptom — its value is contextual. Screen for the treatable drivers rather than accepting "tired" at face value.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Fatigue + pallor + breathlessness** → anaemia
- **Fatigue + cold intolerance + weight gain + constipation** → hypothyroidism
- **Fatigue + low mood + anhedonia + sleep/appetite change** → depression
- **Fatigue + polyuria/polydipsia** → diabetes
- Always ask about sleep quantity and quality and medications (beta-blockers, sedatives) before attributing to disease

---

### 4. Appetite — *"How is your appetite? Are you eating normally?"*

**Why we ask:** Appetite links the constitutional screen to GI, endocrine and psychiatric systems, and modifies the interpretation of weight change (see above).

---

### 5. Lumps and itch — *"Any lumps in the neck, armpits or groin? Any generalised itching?"*

**Why we ask:** Lymphadenopathy is the physical correlate of the lymphoreticular malignancies and chronic infections. Generalised **pruritus without a rash** is a classic paraneoplastic/systemic clue.

**Rules in / rules out:**
- **Rubbery, painless, persistent nodes ± B symptoms** → lymphoma
- **Tender nodes with local infection** → reactive
- **Generalised itch without rash** → lymphoma (esp. Hodgkin), cholestasis, iron deficiency, chronic kidney disease, polycythaemia, thyroid disease

---

## Quick-recall list (drill version)

**WASTING:** weight change (quantified, intentional?) · **THERMOSTAT:** fever, rigors, night sweats · **ENERGY:** fatigue, malaise, sleep · **APPETITE:** appetite/intake · **LUMPS:** lumps/lymphadenopathy, generalised itch
